2026-2027 rules sheets, How to Play pages for all three pools, both-sides rule dropped

Rules pages now carry the sheets supplied 2026-08-25, word for word:

- Gridiron: picks are made in the site account and e-mailed, faxed and phoned
  picks are expressly not accepted (new rule 4), with the account's countdown
  clock named as official. The old rules on nickname/ambiguous designations, on
  invalid picks counting as a loss, and on selecting both sides of the same game
  are gone. 2nd place is $1,500, not $1,550. Contact goes through the message
  board rather than the commissioners' e-mail addresses. 15 rules became 14.
- Drop Dead: picks made in the Drop Dead account; the buy-back moved to rule 4.
- Loser: picks made in the Loser account; the forgot-to-pick default is now the
  VISITING team of the LAST Monday night game of the betting week -- the page
  previously said the first Monday night game.

Code follows the sheets:

- scoring.py no longer looks for both sides of a game. It cost two losses and
  disqualified the entry from the Last Place Award; neither applies now, and
  such a week is no longer treated as penalised for the 0-5 award.
- The Loser missed-pick fallback takes the latest Monday night game by kickoff
  rather than whichever row came back first. It already used the visiting team.

Help pages, replacing the "instructional videos" placeholder:

- /help/gridiron, /help/loser and /help/dropdead are full walkthroughs -- how to
  join and pay, the two deadlines, how to read the pick page, saving and
  removing picks, the situations that catch people out, and a FAQ each.
- Drop Dead covers the one-team-once rule, elimination, and what the $30
  buy-back does and does not restore -- including that it cannot revive an entry
  eliminated for failing to pick.
- Loser covers the point values, that a tie costs you points, and the
  forgot-to-pick assignment.
- Each page shows how to join: the Choose Pools screen with that pool ticked,
  and the join button at the foot of its own rules page. The Gridiron page had
  only a full-page shot of the registration-time screen; it now has both, cropped
  to the part that matters.
- 21 screenshots of the real site in static/help/<pool>/, captured from the
  mid-week-3 testbed with its banners removed. Each sits in a box that scrolls
  sideways on a phone rather than shrinking to nothing.
- templates/_help_shot.html holds the shared figure/FAQ macros so the three
  pages stay consistent.
- main.help_page renders help_<pool>.html when one exists.

Extra accounts no longer ask for a password

Adding a second or third entry now needs only a username. The new account
starts on the password of the account that created it -- the hash is copied, so
the password itself is never handled -- and they stay independent afterwards:
changing one does not change the others. Nothing ever required the passwords to
differ; asking for a second one was friction, and a forgotten one means a
commissioner has to reset an account the player thinks of as theirs. The page
copy said "pick a different username and password", which was wrong on both
counts, and now explains switching accounts from the top-right menu. Creating
one writes an "account_added" row to the activity log.

Local test site: session cookie no longer Secure-only

SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE was unconditionally True, so on the test site -- plain
http on 127.0.0.1 -- browsers that do not make a localhost exception (Safari
among them) dropped the session cookie silently. Every form then posted a CSRF
token with no session behind it and sign-in failed with a misleading "that page
had been sitting open too long". It is now keyed on the database being a testbed
one, the same marker the frozen clock and the seed guard use, so the live site
is unaffected and still Secure-only.

The CSRF error message no longer blames staleness: token expiry is already
disabled (WTF_CSRF_TIME_LIMIT = None), so the real cause is always a missing or
changed session -- usually signing out in another tab.

Email changes:

- Welcome email removed. Registering (and adding another account) no longer
  mails anything -- the player is already looking at the site.
- Weekly picks recap removed: send_picks_recap_email, notifications.py,
  email_weekly_picks.py, the admin email-picks route and the "Email Picks to
  Players" buttons on both week pages. Week.picks_emailed is left on the model;
  dropping a column means rebuilding the table in SQLite and nothing reads it now.
- Message board notifications, both directions. A player posting to the
  commissioners emails every admin with an address on file (bulk send, so one
  bad address does not stop the rest) with the message and a link to the thread.
  An admin replying emails that player with the reply and a link to the board.
  Both go out on a background thread, so a slow mail API never holds up the post.
  The admin flash now says whether the player could be notified.
- Forgot password now says "There is no account with that username or email
  address" instead of the deliberately vague "if that account exists" wording,
  and says so plainly when the account exists but has no address on file.
  Hunter's call. Note the trade-off: the form now confirms to anyone who asks
  whether a given address is registered here.

Gridiron: buy-back removed, signups now close at the Week 1 deadline

The $100 week-2 clean slate that voided week 1 is gone, and with it the reason
late entries were allowed: signups ran to the Week 2 deadline so a latecomer
could buy into week 1 and play 10 picks in week 2. Both are removed, so nobody
can join after week 1 closes and nobody can undo a week.

- helpers: gridiron_signup_deadline is the Week 1 deadline (falls back to the
  season-start Thursday + 2 days at noon). gridiron_buyback_deadline,
  gridiron_buyback_closed and GRIDIRON_BUYBACK_CUTOFF_HOUR deleted.
- scoring: gridiron_buyback_available, gridiron_buyback_catchup_week,
  gridiron_void_through, GRIDIRON_BUYBACK_FEE and GRIDIRON_BUYBACK_PICKS
  deleted. gridiron_pick_limit is 5, or 8 in a makeup week -- never 10.
  gridiron_week_counts no longer voids anything. The award rows drop
  bought_back, and last_place_eligible turns on missed weeks alone.
- models: default_buyback_open no longer opens Gridiron week 2;
  GRIDIRON_BUYBACK_WEEK deleted. Entry.buyback_week stays for Drop Dead.
- blueprints/gridiron: the /buyback route and every buy-back context value are
  gone. The pick page loses the offer card and the "Bought back in" notice.
- Payments and Billing bill no Gridiron buy-back, so the 34 legacy rows in the
  demo database are quoted at $0 and produce no lines at all. The Help hub fee
  table drops the row; the Gridiron How to Play drops the section and now says
  signups close at the Week 1 deadline.
- standings, week_report and the admin week page lose the VOID cells and the
  "buy-back -- 10 picks" captions.
- 15 buy-back tests removed, 3 added: the pick limit can never exceed 8, a week
  is never voided even with a stale buyback_week on the row, and signups close
  at week 1. 32 pass.

Admin Payments page:

- Top table reordered to Gridiron, Loser, Drop Dead, and the Drop Dead column
  renamed "Drop Dead Initial" now that its buy-backs have columns of their own:
  BB1-BB4, one per week the rules allow a buy-back. Green BB is paid, red BB is
  unpaid, dash means that one was never taken.
- Unpaid entry fees now read as a red outlined "Unpaid" badge rather than an
  amber one, in all three pool columns, so what is outstanding reads at a glance.
  The Gridiron week-2 buy-back has no column of its own and stays inline.
- The Paid/Unpaid badges and the BB badges in the By Player table are now
  buttons: clicking one flips it, behind a confirm that names the player, the
  pool and the amount ("Would you like to confirm that Bofa has paid their
  Loser Pool buy in of $20?"), with the reverse direction spelled out as
  marking money NOT received. Buy-back fees are an interchangeable count per
  entry, so clicking a badge settles exactly that fee.
- Each Drop Dead buy-back is now its own BuyBack row (entry, week bought back,
  fee, paid, paid_at, created_at) instead of a pair of counters on Entry that
  treated the fees as interchangeable -- settling the third one used to turn the
  first green. migrate_schema backfills a row per existing buy-back, marking the
  first buy_backs_paid of each entry's as settled, which is what the counter
  meant. Entry.buy_backs_used and buy_backs_paid are still written alongside,
  since the pick page and the printed-rule checks count off them.
- An admin resetting a player's password now writes a "password_reset" row to
  the activity log, filed against that player with the admin named. Somebody
  else taking control of an account is worth being able to find later; the
  temporary password itself is never written to the log. (A player changing
  their own password already logged.)
- Marking anything paid or unpaid writes a "payment" row to the activity log,
  filed against the PLAYER rather than the admin who clicked -- it is money on
  that player's account -- with the admin named in the detail so the trail still
  says who did it. Reads: "Loser Pool entry fee ($20) marked PAID by admin".
- All Entries gains a "Committed" column: when the player took the charge on --
  joining the pool for an entry fee, pressing the buy-back button for a buy-back
  (read from the activity row written at the time). Not when it was paid.

Billing page for players:

- "Billing" in the account dropdown opens /billing: every charge on the account
  -- entry fees and each buy-back as its own line -- with the date it was
  incurred, the amount, and whether the commissioners have recorded it as paid,
  plus owed / paid / season-total tiles and the check-mailing details when
  anything is outstanding.
- It reads the same flags the admin Payments page writes, so marking a fee paid
  there shows up here on the next page load with nothing to keep in sync.
- Billing covers every account sharing the player's email, grouped by account
  with a combined total, since extra lines come out of one pocket and one check
  usually settles them all. A single-account player sees no grouping.
- entry.paid_at and entry.buy_backs_paid_at are new columns (added by
  migrate_schema, idempotent) so the page can say WHEN something was settled;
  the admin toggles stamp them. Rows settled before this existed read "Date not
  recorded" rather than showing a made-up date.
- Buy-back dates come from the activity log written when the button was pressed
  -- the entry row keeps only a count and a week number.
- Entry fees moved to models.POOL_ENTRY_FEES, quoted by both the admin Payments
  page and Billing, so the two cannot drift.

Join flow reworked: the home page is the join page

The tick-box "choose your pools" screen is gone -- the one with JOIN SELECTED
POOLS and Skip for now. Registering, adding an account or switching accounts
now lands on the home page, where all three pools are always shown: one you are
in carries its links plus a green "You're in" and its Make Picks button; one you
are not carries a full-width Join button with the price on it, behind a confirm
that names the amount and says you will owe it to the commissioners. Logged-out
visitors see the price as text, no button.

- auth.choose_pools is now a one-line redirect to main.index, kept because

  templates/auth/choose_pools.html is retired (parked in _to_delete/).
- The three /join routes take a hidden from=home and return to the home page in
  that case, so the remaining Join buttons are in front of the player. Joining
  from a pool's Rules page still lands on its picks, unchanged.
- Gridiron's card shows a closed badge naming the Week 1 deadline once signups

- All three How to Play pages showed a screenshot of the retired screen. They
