📉Loser Pool — How to Play

One pick a week, and you are looking for the team you think will lose. No point spreads, nothing to calculate — just pick a loser. Get it right and you bank that team's points; get it wrong and you lose them.

The short version
1 pick a week
An NFL team you expect to lose. Any team, any week — repeats allowed.
Saturday at noon Eastern
Each team also locks 1 hour before its own kickoff.
$20 per entry
Good for the whole season. More than one entry is permitted.
Most points wins
70% to first, 20% to second, 10% to third.

1. Joining and paying

If you already have a Gridiron Pools account, joining takes one press: open the Loser Pool rules page and press Join the Loser Pool. If you are new to the site entirely, a commissioner sends you an invitation e-mail with a sign-up link, and you are offered all three pools right after you register.

{{ h.figure("loser", "join-button.png", "The Join the Loser Pool button at the foot of the rules page", "The one-press route: the join button at the bottom of the Loser Pool rules page.") }}

Everything happens on the home page. Every pool is listed there, and the ones you have not joined carry a Join button with the price on it:

{{ h.figure("loser", "join-home.jpg", "The three pool cards on the home page, each with a Join button showing its price", "The home page. Press Join Loser Pool — $20 and confirm; the card then shows "You're in" and a button to make your picks.") }}

The entry fee is $20 per entry, good for the whole season. Send a check to Cedric Hay, 12300 US Hwy 19, Hudson, FL 34667. More than one entry is permitted — each extra entry needs its own account and its own $20.

Signups close at the Week 1 deadline — Saturday noon Eastern of the opening week. After that the join button disappears for the rest of the season, for everyone. All three pools work the same way, so if you are thinking about playing, get in before the season starts.

2. How scoring works

Every NFL team is worth a fixed number of points, set before the season and unchanged all year. Bad teams are worth few points; good teams are worth many. The full table is on the rules page, and the number sits on every button when you pick.

Your team loses

You were right. Add that team's point value to your total. Picking a strong team to lose is worth more precisely because it is harder to get right.

Your team wins or ties

You were wrong. That same point value is deducted from your total. A tie counts against you the same as a win does.

Highest total at the end of the 18-week regular season takes first place. There is no elimination in this pool — a bad week costs you points, not your entry, and you can go negative and climb back.

3. Making your pick

Sign in, press Make a Pick in the top menu and find the Loser Pool section. Every game in the week is listed with both teams as buttons. The number on each button is what that team is worth:

{{ h.figure("loser", "pick-grid.jpg", "The Loser Pool pick grid with each team shown as a button carrying its point value", "Both sides of every game, each with its point value. Pick the one you think will lose.") }}

Press the team you expect to lose, then press Save Pick. Nothing counts until you save. Your saved pick then shows on its own, with the matchup underneath it:

{{ h.figure("loser", "locked-pick.png", "A saved Loser Pool pick showing the team, its point value and a remove button", "A saved pick. Press × Remove pick to take it back while the game is still open.") }}

One pick per week — there is no partial credit and nothing to fill in beyond the single team.

Changing your mind

Press × Remove pick, confirm, and you are free to pick someone else. That works right up until the weekly deadline or that team's own kickoff window, whichever comes first. After that the button disappears and the pick is final.

4. Deadlines

The weekly deadline

Saturday at noon Eastern. The countdown clock at the top of the pick page is the official one.

Each team's own lock

A team closes 1 hour before its game kicks off. Thursday night teams are gone by Thursday evening.

Forget to pick and the site picks for you. If no pick is saved by Saturday noon, the rules assign you the visiting team of the last Monday night game of that betting week — and it is scored like any other pick, for better or worse. Missing a week does not end your entry, but it does hand your fate to a team you did not choose.

5. Special situations

A tie

A tied game counts as your team not losing, so the points come off your total, exactly as if they had won.

Picking the same team twice

Perfectly legal here. Any team may be selected any week, as often as you like. (That is the Drop Dead Pool you are thinking of, where each team is usable once.)

College games

NFL only in this pool, so college games never appear on the Loser pick page even in weeks when Gridiron Investments is full of them.

A cancelled or postponed game

Message the commissioners on the board. They will tell you whether to re-pick, and they can clear a pick that can no longer be played.
