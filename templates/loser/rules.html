NO POINT SPREADS . . . JUST PICK LOSERS!

Here's how it works . . .

  • Pick one NFL team to LOSE each week of the 18 week regular NFL season. Any team may be selected any week. (NFL only please)
  • For each correct LOSER you select, you will receive and accumulate points (points are determined by which team you select to LOSE -- see below).
  • If your team wins or ties, its point value will be deducted from your accumulated point total.
  • Highest number of points accumulated at the end of the season wins 1st place - 70% of take, 2nd - 20%, 3rd - 10%.
