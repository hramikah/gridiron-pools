Want more than one team? You can hold several entries — in a single pool, or spread across all three — and we have made them simple to set up and quick to move between.

Each team is a separate entry with its own picks, its own record and its own line in the standings — and its own fee. On this site each one needs its own account, and they all sit under the same email address so you can hop between them without signing out.

Creating another team
  1. Sign in to the account you already have.
  2. Open the menu at the top right that reads “Hi, <your name>” and choose + Add Another Account.
  3. Pick a username for the new team — that is the name everyone sees on the standings, so make it one you will recognise (plenty of people just add a number to the end of the first one).
  4. Press Create Account. There is no second password to choose: the new account uses the one you just signed in with.
{{ shared_figure("add-account.png", "The Add another account page, with a single username field and a Create Account button", "All it asks for is a username. The new team uses the same email and the same password as the account that made it.") }}

Not seeing + Add Another Account in the menu? The commissioners set how many teams each email may hold, and yours is at its limit. Ask on the Message Board and they can raise it.

You are signed into the new team as soon as it is created, on the join page, ready to join whichever pools that team is playing. Joining is per team, so a second team is not automatically in the same pools as your first — you choose again, and each entry is charged separately.

Extra teams close when the pools do. Signups end at the Week 1 deadline, and once they have passed the Add Another Account option disappears, because a new team would have nothing to join. If you want more than one team, set them all up before the season starts.
Switching between your teams

You are only ever signed into one team at a time — that is how the site knows whose picks it is saving. To move between them, open that same “Hi, <your name>” menu at the top right. Your other teams are listed at the top under Switch account; click one and you are in it straight away, no password and no signing out.

{{ shared_figure("switch-accounts.png", "The Hi menu open, listing the player's other accounts under Switch account, above the Add Another Account item", "Your other teams sit at the top of the menu under Switch account. The name in the menu bar always tells you which team you are in.") }}
Check the name before you pick. The menu always shows which team you are signed into. Picks are saved against that team, so the usual mix-up is making two weeks of picks for the same team and none for the other. Switch first, then pick.

Money is the one thing that is not separate: your Billing page — also in that menu — shows everything owed across every team on your email, each entry listed separately with one combined total. It reads the same whichever team you open it from, so there is no hunting around to find what you owe.
