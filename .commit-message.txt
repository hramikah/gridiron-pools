FBS-only college filter, and drop the Drop Dead week-by-week grid

## FBS vs FBS only (the reason the publish timer can come back on)

The college odds feed is americanfootball_ncaaf, which prices FBS-vs-FCS
games alongside the real slate -- Week 1 came back with 85 college games,
most of them 30-to-56-point mismatches. That is why the Thursday publish
timer has been off since early September: re-enabling it without a filter
would flood the board again and re-create any college game deleted by hand.

New fbs.py holds the 136-team FBS list plus the spelling variants a book
might send ("Hawai'i", "Southern California", "North Carolina State"), and
matches on a normalized form so accents and punctuation cannot miss.
publisher.py skips a college game unless BOTH teams are on the list.

A whitelist can only fail by being out of date, and an out-of-date whitelist
loses a real game quietly. So every name that caused a skip is reported:
publish_week() returns skipped_college and non_fbs_names, publish_week.py
writes them to logs/publish_week.log, and publish_this_weekend.py prints
them. A real FBS school appearing there means fbs.py needs its name.

New scripts/check_fbs_filter.py + check-fbs-filter.command pull the live
college feed and print what would be published, what would be skipped, and
every unrecognized name -- read only, writes nothing. Run it before turning
the timer back on.

NFL is untouched: the filter is gated on sport == "college", so Drop Dead
and Loser (straight-up NFL, never college) cannot be affected.

Verified: 75 tests pass, including 7 new ones in tests/test_fbs_filter.py --
FCS games skipped, the FBS side of a mismatch not reported as a whitelist
gap, NFL published to all three pools unchanged, and a re-publish that does
not resurrect a skipped game.

## Master Standings: drop the Drop Dead week-by-week grid

Removed at Hunter's request. It was the last of the three per-week grids on
that page -- the Gridiron and Loser ones went earlier -- and the only caller
of dropdead_matrix(), so the standings route no longer builds any matrix at
all. Each one walked every pick of every entry for every unlocked week, which
was the most expensive work on the richest page of the site.

The Drop Dead tab keeps its standings table (place, player, alive/out,
bought-back badge). Week History and /dropdead/standings cover the same
week-by-week ground for anyone who wants it.

Also drops the now-unused unlocked_week_numbers and all_weeks_data from the
template context.

dropdead_matrix() itself stays in scoring.py, unused, alongside
gridiron_matrix() and loser_matrix() -- which have been in exactly that state
since their own grids were dropped.

Verified: /standings renders 200 with all six tabs intact and the page
well-formed (tag stack balanced, 39 divs open and closed), the Drop Dead pane
down to its one table, mid-week and after the deadline.
