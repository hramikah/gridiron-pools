Switching between your entries

You are only ever signed into one entry at a time — that is how the site knows whose picks it is saving. To move between them, use the menu at the top right of every page, the one that reads “Hi, {{ current_user.username }}”.

Open it and your other accounts are listed at the top, under Switch account. Click one and you are in it immediately — no password, no signing out. The name in that menu always tells you which entry you are looking at, so check it before making picks.

Each entry keeps its own picks, its own record and its own standings position. They never share picks, so you can back completely different teams in each one.

One bill for all of them

Money is the exception: your Billing page — also in that same top-right menu — shows everything owed across every account on your email, not just the one you are signed into.

Each entry is listed separately so you can see what belongs to which, with one combined total at the top. It reads the same whichever account you open it from.
