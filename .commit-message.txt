Standings settle missed weeks first; a Gridiron preseason week acts like a real one

1. The Make a Pick badge and the Master Standings could show a player two
   different places. They run identical code -- the difference was timing.
   process_due_weeks() writes down a past-deadline week's penalties, and it was
   wired into the three pick pages, the admin pages and the two-hourly
   update_scores job, but not into /standings, the three per-pool standings
   pages, or the home page carrying the "Nth of N" badge beside Make Picks.
   Those pages only displayed whatever the last of the others had left behind.
   All five now call it as their FIRST statement -- first because
   helpers._read_cache() memoises reads for the rest of a GET on the assumption
   nothing writes during one, so the write has to land before any cached read.

2. A Gridiron preseason week now behaves end to end like a regular one.
   process_missed_picks records a GridironMiss for a preseason week an entry sat
   out. That single row is what makes the rest work: Rule 8 forgives a FIRST
   failure, so the record stays 0-0-0 rather than 0-5, the standings' Penalties
   column can name the week, and the 8-pick makeup week follows. Recorded but
   never fatal -- the bench check stays gated on counts_for_season(), so a trial
   week cannot end anyone's season. Drop Dead elimination, the spent-team rule
   and the Loser auto-pick are all untouched.

   Empty slots are charged in preseason too, which they always were everywhere
   except the season Won/Lost columns: the "last week W-L-T" column beside them,
   the weekly picks grid and the pick page all call _gridiron_week_empty_losses
   directly and never had that filter, so one row read 0-0 on the left and 0-5
   on the right. A full no-show still costs nothing, because a forgiven first
   miss returns 0 -- what is charged is a part-filled week, exactly as in the
   regular season.

3. Two orderings keyed off the raw week number, which sorts preseason weeks
   (101+) AFTER week 18 even though they happen before week 1:

   - gridiron_first_miss_week() let any regular-season miss beat a preseason
     one, forgiving the wrong week and hanging the makeup off the wrong week.
   - due_weeks() settled week 1 before Preseason Week 4, despite "oldest first"
     being the entire point of that ordering.

   Both now sort in Python with helpers.week_sort_key.

4. gridiron_makeup_week() returns the next week ON THE SCHEDULE instead of
   first_miss + 1. A miss in Preseason Week 4 (104) is followed by Week 1, not
   by a week 105 that will never exist -- which would have granted the makeup
   allowance for a week nobody could ever play.

Verified against a 124-entry replica of the live Preseason Week 4: the 58
non-pickers show 0-0-0, "Preseason Week 4" in Penalties, 8 picks and 2 penalty
slots waiting in Week 1, and none of them benched. 54 tests pass.

5. The makeup week's 2 penalty losses are charged as soon as that week is
   known, instead of waiting for its deadline. They were already shown as slots
   on the pick page from the moment the week opened, so an entry read 0-0-0 on
   the Thursday and 0-2-0 at Saturday noon while the page in front of them said
   2 losses the whole time. Same disagreement as everything else in this commit,
   one function further along. (Commissioner's call, 2026-08-30: on entering the
   makeup week the record already reads 0-2-0.)

   Charged when the league ENTERS the makeup week, via a new
   helpers.week_started() -- the same boundary get_current_week() moves on.
   Not on the week row existing: all 18 regular weeks are created at once, so
   that would have shown the penalty weeks before the week could be played.

   New _week_order() puts bare week NUMBERS in schedule order -- preseason
   weeks are stored at 101+ but come before week 1 -- and gridiron_counted_picks
   and gridiron_penalty_losses now use it, so preseason picks and penalties stop
   dropping out of every through-week view.

Confirmed against the live shape with Preseason Week 5 created and PW4 closed:
a PW4 no-show reads 0-2-0 in the Master Standings, "Preseason Week 4" in the
Penalties column, 0-0-0 in the PW4 column, and gets 8 picks plus 2 penalty loss
slots on the PW5 pick page.

6. A week now opens 72 hours before its deadline instead of 48
   (WEEK_OPENS_DAYS_BEFORE_DEADLINE 2 -> 3), so it becomes the current week at
   noon on the WEDNESDAY before its Saturday-noon deadline rather than the
   Thursday. 2026 Week 1 has a Thursday game, and opening the same day left
   almost no room. The constant drives get_current_week() and week_started()
   together, so the pick pages, the current-week label and the makeup week's
   2 penalty losses all move on the same instant.

7. A preseason Loser Pool week now assigns the no-show auto-pick, and waits for
   its Monday night game like any other week.

   process_missed_picks short-circuited on every preseason week, so an entry
   that forgot a preseason Loser week was left with no pick at all -- pressing
   Process Missed Picks handed out nothing. Preseason weeks now settle down the
   same path as the rest; the pools that must not act on one say so inside
   process_missed_picks instead of at the door.

   Drop Dead keeps its exemption, moved into the branch that needs it:
   elimination ends a season with no way back, so a preseason no-show is left
   alone there, exactly as score_dropdead_pick already leaves a losing preseason
   pick alone. Benching in Gridiron stays gated the same way.

   The MNF wait now applies to preseason too, which is the point: a week whose
   MNF game is missing, unflagged, or carries only typed-in team names (no
   away_team_id) stays owed and visibly unsettled rather than closing quietly
   with the no-shows left pickless.

   diagnose-loser-mnf.command (new, read-only) reports, per Loser week, which
   game is flagged MNF, whether it is linked to real Team rows, how many entries
   have no pick, and which team would be handed out.

8. Settling past-deadline weeks no longer rides on the score fetch succeeding.

   update_scores.py ran update_scores() and sys.exit(1)'d on any exception,
   before it ever reached process_due_weeks(). Since that timer is the only
   thing that settles a week when nobody is on the site, one bad Odds API call
   -- expired key, timeout, monthly credits gone -- left every past-deadline
   week owed until an admin happened to open a page. The two jobs now run, log
   and fail independently; the exit code still reports if either did.
