Standings: hold the current week until its deadline

Two changes to how the standings are ordered and what they are allowed to
show. Both are player-facing and both are on every pool.

1. NOTHING FROM A WEEK REACHES THE STANDINGS UNTIL ITS DEADLINE PASSES

Games kick off Thursday; picks stay open until Saturday noon. Until now the
season tables counted every scored pick the moment it was graded, so a
Thursday-night final moved the table and anyone watching it could read how
the field was doing while their own picks were still open. Drop Dead was
worse than the numbers: dropdead/standings.html prints every entry's picks
with win/loss colouring, and the By Player tab on Master Standings listed
any selected player's current picks outright.

Scoring is untouched -- picks are still graded the moment a game goes final,
eliminations still land, the admin pages and the diagnose scripts still see
live data. It is only the release to shared views that waits.

  standings_visible_weeks(season, pool) -> (week_ids, week_numbers)

is the one definition, keyed on week_unlocked() so it draws the same line
picks already do. Weeks are per-pool, so each pool holds and releases on its
own deadline. Applied in standings_gridiron, standings_loser,
standings_dropdead, gridiron_matrix, dropdead_matrix, loser_matrix,
player_pick_history and gridiron_award_rows.

Drop Dead needed more than a filter: is_active and eliminated_week live on
the Entry and are written the moment a game goes final. _VisibleEntry wraps
an entry with the held-back status AND a picks list already cut to the
released weeks, so a template that reaches for either cannot render this
week by accident. Fail-closed on purpose -- that is what the leaking picks
column was.

An entry knocked out on Thursday is still told immediately on its own home
card, so the buy-back window is not eaten. It is the shared table that
waits.

The makeup week's 2-game penalty now waits with the rest of its week rather
than appearing on the Thursday (gridiron_penalty_losses(visible_only=True)).
The pick page still shows those two slots from the moment the week opens --
that is where the player needs to see them, and it was never the leak. This
narrows the 2026-08-30 timing call to the pick page only.

Every standings page now carries a notice naming the held week and when it
lands, so a table that hasn't moved since Monday doesn't read as broken.

2. A TIE RANKS ABOVE NO RECORD AT ALL

Reported separately: 0-0-1 entries scattered among the 0-0-0 ones. A push is
neither a win nor a loss, and all three Gridiron sorts keyed on wins and
losses only, so both records produced the key (0, 0) -- equal as far as the
sort was concerned, and Python's stable sort then left them in entry-id
order. The same two columns were the whole rank key, so both printed the
same place number.

Ties are now the last tiebreak in the sort order and in the rank key: most
ties wins a level W-L. 0-0-1 above 0-0-0, 5-3-1 above 5-3-0. Wins and losses
still come first. gridiron_awards is deliberately untouched -- its Last
Place tiebreak orders ties the other way, per the 2026-08-23 reading of the
printed rule.

Still visible after change 1: the window runs from Saturday noon, when a
week is released, until the last game goes final on Monday night.
