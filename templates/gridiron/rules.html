🏈Gridiron Investments

$100 entry fee per line. Payouts are tiered based on total entries (roughly the top 12% of the field is paid), plus 1st/2nd half and specialty awards.

  1. Each participant makes five (5) picks weekly. A pick week runs Thursday through the following Wednesday.
  2. Picks may combine NFL and college games, using lines provided weekly. Picks are against the spread; NFL games may also offer an Over/Under selection (you may pick both the spread winner and the O/U for the same game, but not both sides of the same market).
  3. The season runs 90 total picks across the 18-week NFL regular season, including any college games (bowl games included) played in that window.
  4. Picks are due by Saturday at noon (EST). Games kicking off earlier must be picked at least one hour before kickoff.
  5. Standings are ranked by total correct picks (wins) for the season, with ties broken by fewest incorrect picks.
  6. If you fail to submit picks for a week, that week is automatically scored 0-5, and you're allowed 8 picks (instead of 5) the following week to make up for it. Missing more than 5 weeks benches the entry for the rest of the season.
