Advance to the next week as soon as the last game is final

get_current_week used to hold on the week that just closed until the next
week's betting window opened (Wednesday noon). It now moves on as soon as
every game of the closed week has a final score -- normally early Tuesday,
on the first score update after Monday Night Football -- even if the new
week's games aren't posted yet (commissioner's call). An admin-pinned week
still wins; a week whose games never all go final holds as before.
Tests: tests/test_week_advance.py.
