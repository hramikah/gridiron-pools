🏈Gridiron Investments

Hello football fans! It is time to begin officially preparing for another exciting season of GRIDIRON INVESTMENTS! The first week is coming soon (September 9th). As always, we encourage all returning members and prospective new members to confirm your entries as soon as possible so the administrative work that your humble commissioners must do to prepare for the season can be made more manageable.

Following is a complete set of the up-to-date rules for the coming year. Please direct any comments or questions concerning the rules, the GRIDIRON INVESTMENTS club in general, as well as any changes or additions to your e-mail addresses or other information to your humble commissioners at gridironlines@yahoo.com AND awardsltd@icloud.com.

2026-2027 OFFICIAL RULES:
  1. The entry fee remains One Hundred Dollars ($100.00), per line. Checks should be made payable to Cedric Hay and mailed to 12300 U.S. Highway 19, Hudson, FL 34667. Entry fees must be tendered in full, with a postmark on or before September 14th, 2026 or your entry will be stricken. NO EXCEPTIONS.
  2. Each participant shall make five (5) picks weekly. A week for picking purposes begins on Thursday and ends on Wednesday of the following week.
  3. Picks may be a combination of NFL or college games, for which lines will be provided weekly. All picks must be for the game winner only, with the exception of NFL games, where the Over/Under, if provided, may be selected. Please note that it is permitted to pick both the game winner (point spread) and the Over/Under selection for any given NFL game. When making an Over/Under selection, the pick must designate the specific contest to which the Over/Under selection refers.
  4. When making picks, please designate NFL selections by the team’s nickname (i.e. Titans, Steelers, etc.) Ambiguous picks (Tennessee, Pittsburgh, etc.) will be counted as college selections. In addition, please make sure to leave no ambiguity when picking college teams (i.e. by designating Miami, FL versus Miami of Ohio, etc.).
  5. Please note that the official lines will be provided on a weekly basis. Any invalid picks (games for which a line has not been provided) shall count as a loss.
  6. All picks must be emailed to both commissioners [gridironlines@yahoo.com] AND [awardsltd@icloud.com] by [Saturday at noon EST]. Any games with posted kickoff times before that time may be selected, provided that any such pick must be turned in at least one (1) hour prior to such posted kickoff time appearing on the provided official lines. NO FAXED OR TELEPHONE ENTRIES WILL BE ACCEPTED. Please note that E-MAIL IS THE ONLY METHOD FOR SUBMITTING YOUR PICKS. This is for your protection, as well as ease of administration. As e-mail submissions are time-dated, this method will allow (and we would encourage) you to send back-ups to others in case there ever is a question regarding transmission or timing failure. For all e-mail submissions, your commissioners will make every effort to confirm receipt.
  7. The season consists of ninety (90) picks, coinciding with the eighteen (18) week NFL regular season. Any college games (including bowl games) played during this time (meaning those college games played through the Wednesday following the first regular season NFL game, through the Wednesday following the last regular season NFL game) may be selected.
  8. With the exception of the last week, any entrant who fails for the first time to get his or her picks in by the weekly deadline will have the opportunity to pick eight (8) games the following week, with a two (2) game penalty. A second and/or subsequent failure, or a failure for the last week will result in the loss of all games for that week. In the event any selected games are cancelled or postponed, for any reason, entrants will be allowed to pick a replacement game, provided it is submitted prior to the deadline as set forth in Section six (6), above. If an entrant does not submit a replacement game within said timeframe, the entrant may select a replacement game the following week. Additional directions regarding the submission of backup games during the final weeks of the season will be communicated to members as may then be necessary.
  9. During the season, your commissioners will be providing the official lines for the week and will tabulate results and provide weekly updates on the standings to any e-mail addresses that wish to receive them. If you do not have an e-mail address, please hook up with someone who does so you will know the official weekly lines and to receive your updates. Your commissioners and technician each will receive one (1) gratis entry for handling administrative duties.
  10. All entry money will be placed in trust during the season. An accounting of all administrative expenses, including but not limited to committee meeting expenditures; paper; ink; toner; and other expenses/supplies, will be available for inspection and review at year’s end upon the request of any participant.
  11. Prize payouts vary year to year. A sample payout, based on 127 paid entries, may be as follows:

    2nd$ 1,550.00
    3rd$ 1,000.00
    4th$ 700.00
    5th$ 550.00
    6th$ 400.00
    7th$ 350.00
    8th$ 300.00
    9th$ 275.00
    10th$ 250.00
    11th$ 225.00
    12th$ 200.00
    13th$ 175.00
    14th$ 125.00
    15th$ 100.00
    1st Half Winner$ 200.00(leader through week 9)
    2nd Half Winner$ 200.00(week 10 to week 18)
    Last Place Award$ 100.00
    Most Ties Award$ 100.00
    Most 0-5 Weeks Award$ 100.00
    More or fewer entries shall result in a reapportionment of the above distributions, provided, however, that the number of places to be paid shall be Twelve percent (12%) of the total number of paid entries received, rounded to the next higher number. As an example, if there are 100-104 entries there will be 12 places paid. If there are 105-112 entries there will be 13 paid places. The specific payouts, including specialty awards, will be set by the Rules Committee at its official meeting. In any event, the Rules Committee reserves the right to make changes to the above distribution scheme, in its sole discretion.
  12. To qualify for the Last Place Award, an entrant must have submitted picks for the entire season, with no penalties. For the 0-5 Weeks Award, an entrant may qualify even if penalized at some point during the season, provided that on any given penalized week an 0-5 record will not count toward the entrant’s 0-5 weeks for purposes of award qualification.
  13. Entrants may not select both sides of the same game (both the favorite and the underdog or the over and under). An entrant selecting both sides of the same game will be credited with two (2) losses for those picks and will incur a penalty disqualifying the entrant from the Last Place Award. In addition, should the entrant otherwise have gone 0-5 for the week which included the errant selection, that 0-5 posting will not count towards the Most 0-5 Weeks Award.
  14. Place Awards shall be determined first by the total number of correct picks (wins). This shall include the Last Place Award, which shall be determined by fewest wins (as opposed to most losses). In the event of a tie for any position, the tie will be broken by determining the fewest incorrect picks (losses) or, in the case of the Last Place Award, the fewest correct picks (wins).
  15. Separate Loser and Drop Dead pools will once again be available. The official rules for each of these pools will be the subject of a separate publication. Each of these pools will have a separate $20.00 entry fee, payable as set forth in paragraph one (1), above. The payout for each of these pools will be 70% to the winner, 20% for second, and 10% for third.

Exceptions, appeals, and interpretations of these rules shall continue to be at the discretion of the Rules Committee. The Rules Committee members for 2026-2027 are Steve Diller, Glenn Johnson and Paul Ratkovich.
