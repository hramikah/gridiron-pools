Data page: weekly Wrapped recap

New "Data" nav link (/data). A Spotify-Wrapped style, swipeable card recap
of each finished week across all three pools, about half of it Gridiron:
  Gridiron (9) - crowd favorite, how the league leaned, game of the week,
    Lone Wolf / road less traveled, upset of the week (or biggest cover),
    biggest bust, top of the week + 0-5 club, season leaderboard, movers.
  Drop Dead (5) - body count, most popular pick, Graveyard, gutsiest
    survivor, teams the survivors have already burned.
  Loser (5) - people's loser, the board, jackpot & ouch, leaderboard, movers.
  Plus intro totals, a "Your week" card, and team of the week. Usernames shown.

Behind a passcode while in preview (DATA_PASSCODE_DEFAULT in
blueprints/main.py, overridable by the data_passcode Setting); past the
passcode the most recent week shows as soon as its deadline has passed.

Read-only: wrapped.py only SELECTs. A week publishes on its own once its
deadline has passed and every game is final (fallback: 7 days after the
deadline if a game never goes final). Admins can preview earlier.
Tests: tests/test_wrapped.py.
