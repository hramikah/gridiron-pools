🏈Gridiron Investments — How to Play

Everything you need to know to get in, make your five picks each week, and keep track of where you stand. If you have played by e-mail in past years, the one change that matters is that picks are made here on the site now. The 2026-2027 rules put it plainly: your Gridiron Investments account is the only method for submitting picks, and e-mailed, faxed and telephoned picks are not accepted.

1. Joining and paying

Gridiron Investments is invitation-only. A commissioner sends you an e-mail with a sign-up link; that link is what lets you create an account. If you have not received one, ask a commissioner to send you one — you cannot register without it.

  1. Open the link in your invitation e-mail. It takes you to the registration page with your e-mail address already filled in. Choose a username and a password you will remember. Your username is what everyone else sees on the standings.
  2. Join the pool. Registering drops you on the home page, where all three pools are listed. Press Join Gridiron Investments — $100 and confirm the price. Join Drop Dead or Loser the same way, then or any time later.
  3. Mail your entry fee. $100 per entry, check payable to Cedric Hay, mailed to 12300 U.S. Highway 19, Hudson, FL 34667. It must be postmarked on or before September 14th, 2026 or the entry is stricken — the rules say no exceptions, and they mean it.

Everything happens on the home page. Every pool is listed there, and the ones you have not joined carry a Join button with the price on it:

2. Deadlines — there are two of them

This trips up more people than anything else in the pool, so it is worth being clear:

The weekly deadline

Saturday at noon Eastern. Once it passes, the week is closed — you cannot add, change or remove anything, and whatever you had saved is what counts. The countdown clock in your account is the official one.

Each game's own lock

Every game closes 1 hour before its kickoff, even if that is before Saturday noon. A Thursday night game is gone by Thursday evening.

A game that has passed its own cutoff still appears on the pick page, greyed out, so you can see it was there and why you cannot pick it:

{{ h.figure("gridiron", "pick-locked-too-soon.png", "A game row greyed out with the note Locked - kickoff too soon", "A Thursday night game seen on Thursday evening — still listed, no longer selectable.") }}

A week runs Thursday through the following Wednesday, so the games you are picking on Saturday morning are that Sunday's and Monday's games plus any college games through Wednesday.

3. Making your picks

Sign in and press Make a Pick in the top menu, then find the Gridiron Investments section. Every game with a posted line for the week is listed. Here is one row:

{{ h.figure("gridiron", "pick-row-anatomy.png", "A single game row showing the matchup, kickoff time, line, and four buttons", "Left: the matchup, kickoff time in Eastern, the line, and the over/under. Right: the buttons you press.") }}

The four buttons are two separate choices:

  • Spread pick — the favourite is always on the left with a minus number, the underdog on the right with a plus number. Seahawks -4.5 means the Seahawks must win by 5 or more for that pick to be correct. Ravens +4.5 means the Ravens must either win outright or lose by 4 or fewer. A game with no favourite shows PK (pick'em) — just win the game.
  • Total pick (over/under) — NFL games only. Over 45.5 means the two teams' scores added together must be 46 or more. Under 45.5 means 45 or fewer.
You may take both the spread and the over/under on the same NFL game — that is two of your five picks. The site will not let you take the same one twice, and taking opposite sides of one game is simply two picks that cannot both win, so it is a wasted slot rather than a foul.

Press a button to select it; press Clear Selection if you change your mind before saving. Selected buttons fill in solid, in the team's colours:

{{ h.figure("gridiron", "pick-making-selections.jpg", "Several game rows with some picks selected and filled in", "Two spread picks and one over/under selected. The top game is already locked.") }}

When you are happy, scroll to the bottom and press Save Picks. Nothing is submitted until you do. Saved picks appear in the Locked in so far box at the top of the page, with the line you took recorded next to each one:

{{ h.figure("gridiron", "pick-locked-in-list.png", "The Locked in so far box listing three saved picks with the spread shown", "Three of five saved. The number in the heading is your running count for the week.") }}

You do not have to make all five at once. Come back as often as you like before the deadline and add the rest — the count in the heading tells you how many you still owe.

4. Changing or removing a pick

A saved pick can be taken back any time that game is still open, either from the Locked in so far box or from the game's own row, where it shows as a grey Locked: badge. Press the small red × next to it and confirm:

{{ h.figure("gridiron", "pick-locked-row.png", "A game row showing two locked picks each with a red X button", "Both the spread and the total are taken on this game. Either can be removed on its own.") }}

Removing a pick gives you the slot back, so you can select something else. Once the weekly deadline passes, or once that game is inside its 1-hour window, the × disappears and the pick is final.

5. Special situations

You miss a week

The first time you fail to get picks in by the deadline, you get one makeup: the following week you may pick 8 games instead of 5, with a 2-game penalty charged against you from the start. The site does this automatically — your pick page simply shows 8 slots and the two penalty losses:

{{ h.figure("gridiron", "penalty-week.jpg", "A makeup week showing 8 of 8 picks plus two penalty losses", "A makeup week: eight picks made, two penalty losses already on the board.") }}

A second missed week — or missing the final week — is a straight loss of all five games for that week, with no makeup. Miss five weeks and the entry is benched for the season.

Ties and pushes

If the result lands exactly on the number — a 3-point favourite that wins by exactly 3, or a total that lands exactly on 45 when the line was 45 — the pick is a push. It counts as neither a win nor a loss. Pushes are tracked separately and there is a Most Ties Award for whoever collects the most of them.

Cancelled or postponed games

If a game you picked is cancelled or postponed, you may pick a replacement, as long as you do it before that week's deadline. If the deadline has already gone by, you get the replacement pick the following week instead. Message the commissioners on the board so they know.

6. Where results show up

  • Master Standings — every entry in every pool, all in one table.
  • Weekly Picks — who picked what, once a week's deadline has passed.
  • Final Scores — the scores each week's results were scored from.
  • Message Board — announcements from the commissioners, and where you can send them a private message.
