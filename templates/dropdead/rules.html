💀Drop Dead Pool

The object is to be the last guy or gal standing. $20 entry fee per line: 70% to first place, 20% to second, 10% to third.

  1. For each week of the NFL regular season, one NFL team is selected to win straight up with NO point spread.
  2. Each of the 32 NFL teams may be selected ONLY ONCE during the 18 week season.
  3. If your selected team loses or ties, or if you fail to turn in a pick by Saturday at noon (EST), you're done — unless you buy back in.
  4. The competition ceases when there remains only one player standing, or, when fewer than 5 players remain and all lose in a week, the pool concludes at the end of that NFL week. If 5+ remain and all lose, the competition continues. Ties at the end are split pro-rata.
  5. If a tie remains after the regular 18-week season, survivors continue into the playoffs using the 12 playoff teams (each usable once). A tie after the Super Bowl splits the pool equally.
  6. Buy-back: any entrant who would be eliminated in weeks 1-4 may revive their entry for a $30 fee per buy-back. Teams already used remain unavailable.
  7. Picks are due Saturday at noon (EST).
