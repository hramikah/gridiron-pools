How to pay

All entry fees are paid by check. Nothing is paid through this site — the site tracks what you owe, the commissioners handle the money.

Where to send it

Make the check payable to:

Cedric Hay

Mail it to:

12300 U.S. Highway 19
Hudson, FL 34667
Postmarked on or before September 14th, 2026. The Gridiron rules say entry fees must be tendered in full by then or the entry is stricken — no exceptions. One check can cover all your entries; just note which pools it is for.
What it costs
🏆 Gridiron Investments $100 per line
📉 Loser Pool $20 per entry
💀 Drop Dead Pool $20 per line
💀 Drop Dead buy-back $30 each, weeks 1–4

You can play any or all three pools — they are separate entries and separate fees. A second line in a pool needs a second account and a second fee.

Buy-backs are billed the same way. Pressing a buy-back button on the site revives your entry straight away; the fee is then owed to the commissioners along with everything else. No money changes hands on the site.

Anything else

Questions about the rules, your entries, a scoring mistake, or a change to your details all go to the commissioners through the Message Board. For anything binding — payouts, awards, penalties — each pool's official rules are the last word.
